Troubleshooting

Find the problem you see, then follow the link to the fix.

Meter data

  • My interval data file won’t import - Check the format, coverage and missing hours.
  • The annual electricity use looks doubled or halved - The kW and kWh units are mixed up.
  • Some readings don’t represent actual energy use - Exclude them as missing data.

Baseline data

  • My building isn’t on the map - Use a nearby footprint and edit it.
  • My building is in Canada - Draw the footprint yourself.
  • My utility rate isn’t listed - Use an average rate, and ask us to add yours.
  • My baseline went back to “Baseline in Preparation” - A saved change to its inputs needs a new calibration. Check inputs and run it again.

Calibration

  • The Check inputs button is grayed out
  • I need to stop a calibration
  • CV(RMSE) or NMBE is outside ASHRAE Guideline 14
  • “Calibration failed”
  • An “Outdated Baseline” label is shown - Rerun the calibration.

Benchmark

  • My baseline isn’t in the list - Only pinned, calibrated baselines are listed. Pin it on the Baselines tab.
  • No ENERGY STAR score is shown - Some building types aren’t covered by ENERGY STAR.
  • I can’t change the energy code - Only the baseline’s analyzer can.
  • “The AI analysis didn’t finish”

Energy audit

  • My baseline isn’t in the list - Only pinned, calibrated baselines are listed.
  • “Not subscribed” or “Complete Your Energy Audit” - Subscribe the baseline.
  • “The report is outdated”
  • Calculate shows an error
  • Calculate is grayed out
  • Update or Post is grayed out - Calculate and Update first.

Sharing

  • The client doesn’t see the audit
  • Download Full Report is grayed out for a client

Costs and incentives

  • There’s no Save changes button on Retrofit Costs - The standard dataset can’t be edited. Create your own table.
  • Incentives can’t be applied - Incentives need a utility rate, not an average rate.
  • My utility’s incentives are missing - Contact us from the Utility Incentives page.
  • A newly posted building isn’t in the Dashboard totals - Turn on its switch.

Account and billing

  • My total is over $2,400 - Subscribe the baselines in two payments.
  • Cancel subscription is grayed out - Only the subscriber (the baseline’s owner) can cancel.
  • I forgot my password
  • I can’t see the audits shared with me - Sign in as a client with the invited email.

Still stuck? Contact support.
