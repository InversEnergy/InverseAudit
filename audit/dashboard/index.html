Dashboard

The Dashboard totals the savings of your posted audits, so you can see them across your portfolio.

Where: Reporting › Dashboard

Post at least one audit. The Dashboard lists only posted audits of pinned baselines you own or analyze. See Calculate, update and post.

Posted Buildings

Each posted building has one row:

ColumnMeaning
Operation Score, Asset ScoreBefore (Pre) and after (Post) the retrofit
# of ECMsNumber of measures in the audit
Energy Costs AAnnual energy cost today
Costs Saving BAnnual cost savings after the retrofit
Cost of Measures CTotal retrofit cost
Incentive Amount DUtility incentives
Net Project Costs C-DRetrofit cost after incentives
Payback Period C/B, Net (C-D)/BYears to pay back, without and with incentives

Use Copy, Print, Excel or PDF to export the table, and Column visibility to hide columns.

Total savings

The tiles at the top add up the buildings whose switch is on in the first column:

  • Site energy savings in MBtu, with the total floor area
  • Cost savings in dollars, and per ft²
  • Source energy savings in MBtu, and kBtu/ft²
  • GHG emissions reduction in metric tons of CO₂, and kg/ft²

Below them, the savings are split by energy source: electricity, natural gas, fuel oil, district chilled water and district steam, each with its cost savings.

A newly posted building’s switch starts off. Turn it on to include the building in the totals.
